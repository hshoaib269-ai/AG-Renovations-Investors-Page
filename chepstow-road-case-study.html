Full Details

The deal, start to finish

Acquisition

30 Chepstow Road was purchased at auction, for cash, with Garage 112 included in the lot. The flat is share-of-freehold, transferring on completion — which makes the subsequent lease extension significantly cheaper than a standard statutory extension on a purely leasehold flat.

Scenario analysis

Four refurbishment and ARV scenarios were modelled for this property, ranging from a £35K refurb against a £220K ARV up to a £40K refurb against a £240K ARV. A&G is progressing with the higher-finish, £240K ARV target, on the basis that the uplift in sale price outweighs the modest increase in refurbishment spend.
