Full Details

The deal, start to finish

Acquisition

27 Cotelands was bought off-market, for cash, at a discount to comparable local sales. It came with a short lease, which A&G extended during the works. That removed what would otherwise have been the single biggest obstacle for a mortgage buyer.

Refurbishment

The renovation was carried out by A&G's own team and covered a full bathroom refit, a full kitchen refit, electrical work and decoration throughout. The same people and the same standards used on A&G's client projects were applied here.
